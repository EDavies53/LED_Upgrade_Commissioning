The commissioning of Traffic Signals can be divided into two parts

1 The checks that can be carried out before the controller is switched on
2 The checks that can be carried out after the controller is switched on.

Part 1 should have been carried out in accordance with TR 2500A data sheets and is not covered in this document.

Part 2 can be carried out in the absence of the signal company Engineer and it is suggested this is carried out before he arrives on site. One hour upwards should be allowed for this.

Part 3 should be carried out in the presence of a signal company Engineer.

PART 3 ON SITE - CONTROLLER SWITCHED ON

23 Prior to switching controller on, flash out each vehicle lamp, pedestrian green man, pedestrian red man and wait indicator to ensure that they illuminate when activated by the Traffic Signal Engineer.
24 Switch lamps on and check that correct traffic movements take place in each stage. Be ready to switch lamps off if unexpected traffic movements take place and investigate accordingly.
25 Check that all lamps on each approach are operating and in the correct sequence.
PUFFINS & NEAR SIDE TOUCAN - No signal for 12 seconds followed by red man and green to vehicles with latched pedestrian demand. FAR SIDE TOUCANS - No signal for 12 seconds followed by red man and green to vehicles with latched pedestrian demand.
26 Check that all far-sided/near-sided pedestrian aspects are working and in the correct sequence.
27 Check operation of signal dimming by covering PE cell and observing signal aspects. Dimming voltage to be 27.5v for ELV sites.
28 Check that signal dimming is wired in (I.e. installation should be permanently bright.)
29 The primary signal heads shall be aligned as follows:- On a high speed approach at a point 1.5m above the carriageway on the centre line 200m from the signals. Otherwise, at a point 1.5m above carriageway on the centre line 50m from the stop line.
30 On each approach the height of the centre of the amber lens of each signal head above the carriageway shall be the same.
31 The secondary signal heads should be angled towards a point 1.5m above the carriageway in the nearside channel of approach 25m from the stop line.
32 Far-sided pedestrian signal heads should be directed to the centre of the crossing at the opposite kerb.
33 Near-sided pedestrian signal heads should be angled at an angle where the aspect is clearly visible at the point where pedestrians normally wait to cross.
34 The illumination of the red and green man aspects shall be uniform.

Controller Operation

35 Check timing from signal aspects as set up in the controller - as in section 21
36 Check operation of all manual switches in the controller. These may include: Lamps On/Off; Continuous Pedestrian/Cycle Demand; Fixed Time Operation (FVP); Continuius Vehicle Extension; LED Fault Test (LED's within maual panel to illuminate)
37 Check that the controller is operating in the correct mode.
38 Check the time clock is set at correct time, day and week number.

Detection

39 Check operation of detector loops by observing operation of detector unit channels. The detector channel L.E.D's should come on as vehicles pass over the detector loops.
40 There shall be separate detector packs or channels for each approach.
41 Check that the VA detector units are set to the appropriate setting for sensitivity and detuning time. (E.g. Sensitivity = High, Presence = 3 to 4 minutes)
42 Check operation and alignment of Above Ground Detectors. The L.E.D. indicator on the AGD should illuminate when vehicles are 39m from stop line (minimum).
43 AGD cables should be neatly zip-tied or wrapped to top signals head bracket, with no cables hanging loose.
44 Check that detector channels & AGD’s are correctly connected to the controller logic by checking the controller or handset display against the detector, unit display or vehicle passage over the detector loops. Ensure all detectors/push buttons are included in the DFM.

Detection

45 Check Vehicle extension times are correct.
46 Ensure AGD's are wired in using plugs and sockets. If the detectors are hardwired in please note as an outstanding issue at the end of this commissioning sheet.
47 Check correct operation under VA control. The controller should be observed to make a minimum change, a gap change and a maximum change where site conditions allow.
48 For sites without SDE, consider the controller is set to PRE-TIMED MAX mode and check operation accordingly. For sites with SDE ensure PRE-TIMED MAX mode is not set.
49 Check push buttons operate and illuminate wait indicators. Buttons should not operate when the green man is showing. The wait indicator should go off when the green man comes on.
50 Check that all Kerbside detectors are set up to detect within the area that is occupied by pedestrians waiting to cross. Ensure at least the push button and the front two rows of the tactile paving are covered by the detectors footprint.
51 Carry out tests to ensure the Kerbside detectors call and cancel pedestrian demands, including a latched test without a kerbside detect.
52 Check that all On-crossing detectors are set up to detect the crossing path and ensure that the road width is covered by the On-crossing detectors.
53 PUFFINS & NEARSIDE TOUCANS - Carry out tests to ensure the On-crossing detectors extend the All Red period.
54 FAR SIDED TOUCANS - Carry out tests to ensure the On-crossing detectors extend the Blackout period.

Speed Control

55 Speed Discrimination (Double SDE) - check for 3 second green extensions for vehicles travelling over 30mph up to phase maximum.
56 Speed assessment - check for 5 second additional fixed green extension for vehicles travelling over 28mph. Following intergreen should extend by an additional 2 seconds.

Audible and Tactile Units

57 Check all audible units operate satisfactorily and adjust volume according to site requirements

Puffin/Toucan Facilities

58 Where tactile signals are provided, check correct operation of all tactile units and that they have been installed on the right hand side of the bottom of the push button unit.
59 Check that the audible signal can be heard from the adjacent crossing point but is not so loud that it causes undue disturbance.
60 Where audibles are provided all pedestrian phases shall display a green man simutaneously and the audible signals shall commence and terminate with the green man.
61 With assistance from signal contractor check within pedestrian demand units or combined nearside units that audible units are installed correctly. Check in particular the audible unit bracket is installed the 'correct way round' as not to cause a short against the wait board

Urban Traffic Control

62 Check that a suitable OTU and router is fitted.
